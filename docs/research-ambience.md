## Remaining work and playback

Seek an independently documented recording of an operating refrigerated shipping container with item-level permission to redistribute and modify. Acquire candidates legitimately, retain source hashes, inspect rights and provenance, listen to originals and final masters, and record the reviewer and results. Prefer stereo for environmental harbour ambience; a single reefer unit may be more useful as a mono spatial point source, but choose channels after listening and retain original channel information. Prepare 48 kHz 16-bit PCM WAV with documented processing, check for clipping, and import through the maintainer command. For either category use a full-event or bounded ambience until the actual loop transition has been auditioned; never promise a seamless loop from duration or waveform checks alone.
