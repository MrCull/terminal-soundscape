## Harbour

- **Candidate for manual acquisition:** [harbor-ambiance.WAV](https://freesound.org/people/L.Finck/sounds/636556/) by L.Finck. The item page identifies a stereo, 48 kHz, 24-bit, 99.192-second harbour field recording and identifies its licence as CC0. This is harbour ambience, but the page does not establish a container terminal or a particular operation. The item page says login to download. Verify the current item terms on acquisition, listen to the complete original and prepared master, and document their hashes and exact conversion before import. Do not assume the ends form a seamless loop.
- **Candidate for manual acquisition:** [Port ambience](https://freesound.org/people/arnaud%20coutancier/sounds/436831/) by arnaud coutancier. The recordist describes a quay recording with gulls, voices, boat motors and work sounds. The item page identifies CC BY-NC 3.0, restricting commercial use; the current workflow accepts only the listed 4.0 attribution licences, so this cannot be imported unchanged through it. The page says login to download. Its people and fishing-boat setting may limit suitability for an industrial container terminal. Obtain, audition and assess before any implementation decision; do not infer the machine identity or a seamless loop.
- **Rejected pending clarification:** [Seaport ambience Vlissingen](https://freesound.org/people/klankbeeld/sounds/393861/) by klankbeeld. Although the item page displays CC BY 4.0, its description also asks users not to redistribute the raw sound as stock material. That conflicts with the intended reusable collection sufficiently to leave rights ambiguous for this use. Do not import without clarification from the rights holder.

## Reefer

- **No verified reefer-container recording found.** Searches for reefer and refrigerated-container recordings did not yield a source with both an identifiable recording and item-specific redistribution terms. A refrigerated container has a cooling unit and associated temperature-control equipment, as described in [DB Schenker's reefer overview](https://www.dbschenker.com/th-en/insights/blog/a-grapes-journey-from-egyptian-fields-to-european-plates-2355772); this does not establish what any candidate sounds like.
- **Possible substitute, not a reefer:** [Refrigerator Hum](https://freesound.org/people/iainmccurdy/sounds/638091/) by iainmccurdy. The item page describes a stereo recording made inside a domestic refrigerator and displays CC BY 4.0. Its stated compressor and refrigerant sounds do not establish the sound of a shipping-container unit. Original download requires login. Treat it only as a possible clearly labelled substitute after acquisition and listening, not evidence of authentic reefer ambience. The creator suggests it loops smoothly, but the actual prepared master and boundary still require a listener's review.

## Remaining work and playback

Seek an independently documented recording of an operating refrigerated shipping container with item-level permission to redistribute and modify. Acquire candidates legitimately, retain source hashes, inspect rights and provenance, listen to originals and final masters, and record the reviewer and results. Prefer stereo for environmental harbour ambience; a single reefer unit may be more useful as a mono spatial point source, but choose channels after listening and retain original channel information. Prepare 48 kHz 16-bit PCM WAV with documented processing, check for clipping, and import through the maintainer command. For either category use a full-event or bounded ambience until the actual loop transition has been auditioned; never promise a seamless loop from duration or waveform checks alone.
