Checked 2026-10-31. The crane candidate described below was pending listening review at the time of this research. See the [manifest](../manifest.json) for the current inventory and review statuses, and the generated [coverage report](../COVERAGE.md) for current reviewed coverage and gaps.

- **Pending candidate:** [WWS Crane.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:WWS_Crane.ogg), Work With Sounds / Technical Museum of Slovenia, recordist Dušan Oblak. The item page describes a five-ton bridge crane at a steel plant, and states CC BY 4.0 with attribution, licence link, and changes indicated. Wikimedia lists a rights-holder permission ticket. This is a plausible industrial substitute, not evidence of a container crane or a specific terminal action. The item page reports 63.996 seconds of Ogg Vorbis at 62 kbps, so fidelity is limited. The original download SHA-256 is `0b673f3560432af138236c00c1f1bf7616f77e01af9f7c094d58466bd59e2d67`. A mono 48 kHz 16-bit PCM WAV derivative is in the collection; the manifest records its hash and the conversion command. No listening was possible in this environment: a reviewer must listen to both source and master and decide whether the candidate is suitable. Do not loop without reviewing the boundary.
- **Candidate requiring account:** [Crane, Noises 06](https://freesound.org/people/LilMati/sounds/396355/) by LilMati. Its item page states CC0 and describes an active crane, but original download requires login. Not downloaded or auditioned; precise crane and operational context remain uncertain.
- **Rejected:** [toy wooden crane noise](https://freesound.org/people/itinerantmonk108/sounds/557268/) is explicitly a toy, not a useful real terminal machine recording.

The [WWS item description](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:WWS_Crane.ogg) explains that its bridge crane lifts and transports chained loads. That describes the recorded equipment, not proof of operating sounds or alarm practice at a container terminal. No alarm guidance is inferred.
