# Vehicle candidate research

Checked 2026-10-31. One agricultural tractor substitute is a pending candidate; no vehicle recording has been reviewed, so vehicle movement remains a reviewed coverage gap.

- **Blocked — authentic terminal tractor:** [Moving the mini terminal tractor backwards.wav](https://freesound.org/people/dibko/sounds/623122/) by dibko. The item describes a small terminal tractor reversing with an audible warning, recorded in Maribor using a Zoom H5. It lists 62.621 seconds, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit stereo WAV and CC BY 4.0, which permits sharing and adaptation with attribution. However, the creator's same item description asks users not to redistribute raw files as stock material. This conflicts with the displayed licence for the intended collection, so permission for redistribution needs clarification before import. The original download also requires a Freesound login. It was not downloaded or auditioned. The specific model and whether the warning is representative of other terminal tractors are unknown; do not generalise this warning to all vehicle operations.
- **Pending substitute - agricultural tractor driving:** [WWS TractorUrsusC342driving.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:WWS_TractorUrsusC342driving.ogg), Work With Sounds / Museum of Municipal Engineering, sound recordist Monika Widzicka. The item describes a 1960s agricultural prototype driving, not a terminal tractor. Its [item page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=File:WWS_TractorUrsusC342driving.ogg&oldid=978580515) identifies CC BY 4.0 and says the rights holder's permission was confirmed in Wikimedia VRT ticket 2014082510012677. [Sounds of Changes identifies the recording and links the same Commons item](https://www.soundsofchanges.eu/sound/tractor-ursus-c-342-driving/), and its [use policy](https://www.soundsofchanges.eu/use-the-sounds/) specifies CC BY 4.0 and permits sharing, adaptation and commercial use with attribution. The 107 kbps stereo Ogg Vorbis source SHA-256 is `18d879e54147e2a6e284d0c0c5a03dda0bc71ec99cf1b0498bb8f29d465a0acd`. FFmpeg downmixed and resampled it to a 91.436-second mono 48 kHz 16-bit PCM master; the manifest records the exact command and output hash. Lossy source and downmix limit fidelity. Automated checks found no full-scale clipping; listening and suitability review remain necessary.
- **Not used - WebM video:** [Tractor Ursus C 342 (driving).webm](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tractor_Ursus_C_342_(driving).webm) identifies the same tractor but its permission summary says CC BY 4.0 while the YouTube licence template specifies CC BY 3.0. Its bot checked YouTube licence status, not ownership. The differing licence statements and uncertainty about underlying rights made the audio-only item above a clearer choice; no WebM derivative remains in the collection.
- **Rejected as a terminal movement recording:** [WWS TractorUrsusC342engine.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:WWS_TractorUrsusC342engine.ogg) is described as an agricultural tractor engine in operation, without establishing vehicle movement. Its item page lists CC BY 4.0 and a Wikimedia permission ticket. It may be useful for an engine study, but does not establish the requested movement event.
- **Account required; weak substitute:** [Diesel Drive by #1 MZ000004.wav](https://freesound.org/people/DAVESTALKER/sounds/443322/) by DAVESTALKER is labelled CC0 and describes a modified Ford F350 pickup driving past. It is not terminal equipment, and its original download requires login. It has not been downloaded or auditioned.

This environment provides no means for the agent to hear and evaluate recordings. A capable reviewer must listen to the original and prepared master, assess suitability and record the review. If a loop is proposed, its boundary must also be heard; no loop is currently justified. For further candidates, resolve item-level redistribution rights and access, retain hashes and validate their masters. The pending substitute is not a reviewed recording or evidence of terminal alarm practice.
