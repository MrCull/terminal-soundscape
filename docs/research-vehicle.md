# Vehicle candidate research

Checked 2026-10-31. No vehicle recording has been accepted or reviewed; vehicle movement remains a coverage gap. The following are research leads, not release-ready assets.

- **Blocked — authentic terminal tractor:** [Moving the mini terminal tractor backwards.wav](https://freesound.org/people/dibko/sounds/623122/) by dibko. The item describes a small terminal tractor reversing with an audible warning, recorded in Maribor using a Zoom H5. It lists 62.621 seconds, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit stereo WAV and CC BY 4.0, which permits sharing and adaptation with attribution. However, the creator's same item description asks users not to redistribute raw files as stock material. This conflicts with the displayed licence for the intended collection, so permission for redistribution needs clarification before import. The original download also requires a Freesound login. It was not downloaded or auditioned. The specific model and whether the warning is representative of other terminal tractors are unknown; do not generalise this warning to all vehicle operations.
- **Possible substitute — agricultural tractor driving:** [Tractor Ursus C 342 (driving)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tractor_Ursus_C_342_(driving).webm), Museum of Municipal Engineering, sound recordist Monika Widzicka, via Sounds of Changes. The item describes an early-1960s agricultural prototype driving, and lists CC BY 4.0; attribution, licence link and indication of modifications are required. It is not a terminal tractor. Its suitability as a substitute and its audio track have not been auditioned, and no file was imported. The compressed video source and extraction would require technical inspection and provenance of the resulting derivative.
- **Rejected as a terminal movement recording:** [WWS TractorUrsusC342engine.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:WWS_TractorUrsusC342engine.ogg) is described as an agricultural tractor engine in operation, without establishing vehicle movement. Its item page lists CC BY 4.0 and a Wikimedia permission ticket. It may be useful for an engine study, but does not establish the requested movement event.
- **Account required; weak substitute:** [Diesel Drive by #1 MZ000004.wav](https://freesound.org/people/DAVESTALKER/sounds/443322/) by DAVESTALKER is labelled CC0 and describes a modified Ford F350 pickup driving past. It is not terminal equipment, and its original download requires login. It has not been downloaded or auditioned.

This environment provides no means for the agent to hear and evaluate recordings. A capable reviewer must listen to the original and prepared master, assess whether each is suitable for the intended event, and record the review. If a loop is proposed, they must also listen to its boundary; no loop is currently justified. Resolve item-level redistribution rights and account access first, retain source hashes, prepare and inspect a 48 kHz 16-bit PCM master using the collection workflow, and validate and regenerate the catalogue. Until then, no candidate is represented as an asset, a reviewed recording, or evidence of terminal alarm practice.
